Mandate

The Mandate

Our mandate is to preach the gospel of Jesus Christ, make disciples, and serve humanity with love and compassion. We pursue prayer, worship and mercy ministry that serves both our neighborhood and communities around the world.

We believe every life matters, every person is loved, and every hand can be extended in service. ASO exists to make faith practical through caring, teaching and outreach.

Mission

Our Mission

Our mission is to be a beacon of hope and a source of transformation in our communities and beyond through evangelism, discipleship, and social outreach.

We gather to worship, commit to prayer, train leaders and care for the vulnerable so that every person can discover purpose, healing and renewed hope.

Vision

Our Vision

Our vision is to see a world where every person has the opportunity to experience the love of Christ and live a life of purpose and fulfillment.

We imagine communities transformed by prayer, courageous service and leaders who shepherd with compassion and wisdom.

Belief

What We Believe

We believe in the Bible as the inspired and authoritative word of God, the Trinity, the deity of Jesus Christ, salvation through faith in Jesus, and the importance of living a life that reflects the love and teachings of Christ.

Everything we do is shaped by grace, truth and community as we build disciples for every generation.
